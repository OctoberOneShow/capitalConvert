# Love and Deepspace character media

The five bundled WebP portraits and optional streamed character videos come from the character section of the [official Love and Deepspace website](https://loveanddeepspace.infoldgames.com/en-EN/home). Character names, designs, portraits, and videos belong to Infold Games / Papergames. This mini-game is a fan-made adaptation with original gameplay, dialogue, and memory vignettes; the dialogue is not official game dialogue.

Forty JPEG and PNG artworks (eight per partner) come from the downloadable
[official English gallery](https://loveanddeepspace.infoldgames.com/en-EN/gallery?type=img)
and [official Chinese gallery](https://deepspace.papegames.com/gallery?type=img),
bringing the local collection to 45 distinct images. The 1.0 collection adds twenty
new artworks, including eight native 3840 × 2160 images and twelve native Full HD
images in landscape or portrait orientation. They are bundled locally so viewing
the collection does not require a network connection.

Official source titles, local filenames, source URLs, pixel dimensions, file sizes,
and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in [gallery-sources.json](gallery-sources.json).
The three new Caleb portraits from the Chinese gallery retain their official
Chinese titles in `sourceTitle`; their English display titles are descriptive
translations marked with `titleIsTranslation`, rather than official localization
names. The images include both character memory artwork and illustrated fantasy
scenes. The downloaded originals are retained without resizing or recompression;
scene crops, viewpoint motion, transitions, and photo overlays are generated by
this fan project. The gallery sources and the distinct artwork were checked on
8 October 2026.

The `thumbs` folder contains 45 generated WebP previews for collection cards and
partner selection. Previews preserve the source proportions, use an edge of at
most 480 pixels, and are encoded at quality 82. The original source files remain
unchanged and provide the full-resolution gallery and photo views.

The five original character portrait responses were WebP, so they are stored with
`.webp` extensions. Portraits load locally, including when the toolkit is opened
directly from disk. Videos stream when the player chooses **Animate character** or
**Say hello**, stay muted, and pause on game switches, drawer close, route or partner
changes, opening the album, and motion-off preferences. A video failure leaves the
local portrait visible.

The Together view also starts the optional muted official video when **Say hello**
is chosen. The photo studio renders crops of the bundled portraits with original
frames, lighting overlays, and stickers; exported PNGs include the media credit.
Claw-machine prizes, the simplified Kitty Cards rules, quality-time timer, and
branching date stories are original fan implementations inspired by the activities
described in the [official app listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/love-and-deepspace/id6443467666).
The project uses portraits and streamed video, rather than the commercial game's
interactive 3D character engine, animation rigs, voices, or date scripts.
