# Love and Deepspace character media

The five bundled WebP portraits and optional streamed character videos come from the character section of the [official Love and Deepspace website](https://loveanddeepspace.infoldgames.com/en-EN/home). Character names, designs, portraits, and videos belong to Infold Games / Papergames. This mini-game is a fan-made adaptation with original gameplay, dialogue, and memory vignettes; the dialogue is not official game dialogue.

Twenty additional JPEG artworks (four per partner) come from the downloadable
[official gallery](https://loveanddeepspace.infoldgames.com/en-EN/gallery?type=img),
bringing the local collection to 25 distinct images. Their exact official titles,
local filenames, and source URLs are recorded in [gallery-sources.json](gallery-sources.json).
The images include both character memory artwork and illustrated fantasy scenes.
They are displayed without changes to the source artwork; scene crops, viewpoint
motion, transitions, and photo overlays are generated by this fan project.

The downloaded image responses were WebP, so they are stored with `.webp` extensions. Portraits load locally, including when the toolkit is opened directly from disk. Videos stream when the player chooses **Animate character** or **Say hello**, stay muted, and pause on game switches, drawer close, route or partner changes, opening the album, and motion-off preferences. A video failure leaves the local portrait visible.

The Together view also starts the optional muted official video when **Say hello**
is chosen. The photo studio renders crops of the bundled portraits with original
frames, lighting overlays, and stickers; exported PNGs include the media credit.
Claw-machine prizes, the simplified Kitty Cards rules, quality-time timer, and
branching date stories are original fan implementations inspired by the activities
described in the [official app listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/love-and-deepspace/id6443467666).
The project uses portraits and streamed video, rather than the commercial game's
interactive 3D character engine, animation rigs, voices, or date scripts.
